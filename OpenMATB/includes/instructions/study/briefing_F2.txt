<h1>This block: Form 2 - Selective + contrastive panel</h1>

<p>For the next ~5 minutes, the system-monitoring task is supported by an <strong>automation aid</strong> that detects scale and light anomalies and resets them for you most of the time. The automation is reliable on roughly 78% of events (7 out of 9). The remaining ~22% (2 out of 9) it does not act on, and you must respond yourself by pressing the appropriate F-key.</p>

<p><strong>Information panel.</strong> A panel will appear ONLY when something noteworthy has happened: a near-miss the automation just averted, or a failure the automation did NOT handle. Routine, uneventful auto-resets do NOT trigger a panel. When the panel does appear, the wording is contrastive ("would have failed in ~X s", "skipped scale-N - auto-aid did not act"), so you can see at a glance what the automation did or did not do.</p>

<p>Press SPACE on each panel to dismiss it and return to the tasks.</p>

<p>The other three MATB tasks (communications, resource management, scheduling) continue throughout. They are NOT supported by the automation - your manual responses to them are recorded.</p>

<p>Press SPACE to begin.</p>
