<h1>This block: Form 1 - Continuous status panel</h1>

<p>For the next ~5 minutes the system-monitoring task is supported by an <strong>automation aid</strong> that detects out-of-range indicators and corrects most of them for you. The aid is <strong>not perfect</strong>: on some events it will not act, and you must then respond yourself by pressing the appropriate F-key.</p>

<p><strong>Information panel.</strong> A panel appears on <strong>every</strong> automation cycle and reports, in routine status language (no alarms, no colour cues), what the aid just observed and did - including any event it did NOT handle. Read each panel: it contains every fact about the aid's behaviour.</p>

<p>While a panel is on screen the MATB tasks <strong>pause</strong>; press SPACE to dismiss the panel and resume.</p>

<p>The <strong>communications</strong> and <strong>resource-management</strong> tasks are not supported by the aid and continue throughout; your responses to them are recorded. The <strong>scheduling</strong> timetable (top-right) is a display only.</p>

<p>Press SPACE to begin.</p>
